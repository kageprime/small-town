The System

VESTIGE talks. Negotiates. Gets bored. Lies.

Not a cold blue window — a parasite with opinions. It claims to be a laugh the Crossroads left behind. No Warden confirms it. No Warden denies it. Drag the Sync slider: more power, more control, blacker veins.

The City

Cinderfall — a capital that moved twice and never finished moving

Fictional sprawl. Terminal-park chaos, a gilt trade maze, stilt slums on black water — built to feel like nowhere on any map. Smell of diesel and roast corn. Sound of horns and generators. And past the expressway, floodlights that never go off.

Factions

Three powers. Two clocks. One boy.

The Table hunts Joss. The Returned harvest him. The Combine is the ground both stand on. Both clocks advance when Joss grows — power draws attention, attention accelerates both. Click to tick (session sketch only).

Chalk • Smuggling • Sabotage

HiddenThe cure tissue works — partially — and the oldest cell rations it. A cured Terminus owes nothing. A dying Terminus owes everything.
GROUND

DOCTRINE

Five-Person Cell

Spire law • Joss breaks it

Vanguard / Lancer / Lantern / Weaver / Chaplain. Legal cells run 4+1. Joss plus his Bound is an illegal one-man cell — the rules break, which is the point, and why everyone is afraid of him.

Bestiary

Things that hunt the climb

Street-faith monsters with readable rules. Each one teaches a mechanic: respect, preparation, attention.

LAWFUL • CLOTH

The Pall

Layers of cloth = HP layers

A tower of layered cloth moving like weather. Never strikes first — ignored, it blocks; insulted, it slams; attacked, the cloth opens and the argument comes out. The greeting obeisance stuns it. Speedrunners hate it. Lore-keepers farm it.

BOSS • 3 LIVES

Returnling

Dies laughing, returns stronger

First life lost and crying. Second laughing and faster. Third quiet, adult-eyed, negotiating. Only a genuine chaplain's rite stops the return — the flux checks sincerity, mercenaries can't fake it. It has been visiting Ward C longer than Joss has been climbing.

UTILITY • TRUTH

Shrine-kept ridgebacks, ears up for truth, folded for lies. One sleeps in the Hold's doorway. It barks at Joss's head whenever VESTIGE speaks. Never explain it in dialogue — let the player notice before Joss does.

MAIN • FLOOR 2 TEST

The Sigil Game

Betrayal scheduled • Rook enforces

Five teams, one endless gala hall. Hold a sigil when the bell rings. The third bell's rule is never announced — it's discovered, usually by the loser: sigils worn by the bound dead count double. Joss is the only player with dead on his side.

  • Hold a sigil at the final bell Alliances form in minutes and rot faster.
  • Win without spending an echo Stay flat. Let someone else bleed for it.
  • Spot the rig The Game is a sieve. Spotting it proves what they're hunting.
  • Survive Rook's rematch — and her unfiled report
